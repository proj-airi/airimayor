# Project city observations from recorded tools

The offline City page projects successful city reads, native statistics history, write ledgers, actions and returned images inside the existing recording worker. The city observation module exposes a cursor-scoped `view`; the host returns bounded chart points and paged readings/actions through the existing worker interface. A new periodic sampler was rejected because existing tools already record the city's facts and clock anchors, and another capture path would change what is observed and duplicate the persisted contract.

City observations are available only after their source record is reached. A native statistics response can reveal earlier samples at their original frames; it cannot reveal them before that response. Current cards use the last observation at or before the selected city frame and retain its date and evidence. Dates label exact recorded clock anchors; other native history frames remain explicit without inferred dates or filled values. Overlapping history samples use the latest acquired reading for the same metric/frame. Untimed reads remain inspectable in Evidence. Finance rates retain their monthly units, demand factors remain signed, and absent fields are not zero-filled. An exhaustive citywide notification count snapshot does observe zero for a previously seen type no longer present; its filtered and capped detail rows never replace those counts.

Road traffic series retain entity index and version, so changing or truncated road lists cannot become a citywide traffic average. Writes show their original before/after differences and the shared ledger judgment, including failed writes that changed the city. Clock advances use their completion anchor; other reads use their application anchor, or completion when application is unavailable. Images load on demand from the latest acquired map/screenshot at the selected city time. Choosing evidence seeks to its exact source record. This extends [0054](0054-offline-recording-analysis.md): Metrics and Search cover the recording, while City follows acquired knowledge at the replay cursor.

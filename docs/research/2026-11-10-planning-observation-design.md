## What the current mayor receives

The playbook already asks the mayor to plan for the whole city, compare two or three directions or densities, explain the choice, and prefer real gaps over repeating the previous action. Expansion should begin with fresh citywide imagery; road hierarchy, alternative collector paths, freight access, topology checks, and fresh city reads after a batch are explicit. Repetitive expansion alone therefore does not establish that instructions were absent. Sources: [AgentSystemPrompt.cs:46](../../Mod/Host/AgentSystemPrompt.cs#L46), [49](../../Mod/Host/AgentSystemPrompt.cs#L49), [66–96](../../Mod/Host/AgentSystemPrompt.cs#L66-L96).

These instructions do not enforce a city-planning gate. City functions pass calls to the city tools; native validation decides construction legality, not whether a growth direction serves the city. Native `todos_*` functions hold the plan. Their mutations emit `plan.changed` with titles, descriptions and completion; the chat projection reads titles and completion at the end of a turn. A visible checklist is evidence of a declared plan, not evidence that alternatives were considered. Sources: [CityFunctions.cs:46–68](../../Agent/CityFunctions.cs#L46-L68), [HarnessTrace.cs:75–105](../../Agent/HarnessTrace.cs#L75-L105), [ClientSession.cs:270–277](../../Agent/ClientSession.cs#L270-L277).

Visual access needs verification in the actual request. The built-in mayor offers visual tools only when both the visual permission and the resolved model's image capability are true. Catalog image capability comes from input modalities. Prompt composition removes image instructions only when the permission is off. Consequently, permission on plus a catalog model without image input can leave image instructions in a session whose catalog omits `map_image`; this is a conditional source finding, not a confirmed property of this run's selected connection. `map_image` is a geometry overview, without names or transit-route overlays. An omitted extent produces a citywide frame from collected geometry; bounded requests show a site. `map_text` instead reports terrain, ownership/buildability, regions, directional sectors and road topology for a requested range, including omitted metadata. A broad text view can locate alternatives; bounded views can inspect them. Neither map approves construction. Network lists return at most 64 rows and topology findings are capped at 64, so absence from a capped list is not proof of citywide absence. ## What summaries preserve and what the record exposes

The pinned MAF 1.27.0 native summarizer runs through the in-memory history reducer; the separate in-flight compaction provider is disabled. It uses the selected client and default native summary prompt, with output capped at `min(selected output limit, 4096)`. Reduction persists for subsequent model calls within that loaded session. It is not a game-restart chat-resume mechanism. Sources: [Agent project:14](../../Agent/airimayor.Agent.csproj#L14), [ClientSession.cs:59–98](../../Agent/ClientSession.cs#L59-L98), [RecordedCompaction.cs:21–30](../../Agent/RecordedCompaction.cs#L21-L30), [ADR 0053](../adr/0053-native-summary-checkpoints.md).

With a 128,000 context budget and 16,384 output reserve, compaction triggers above 55,808 estimated history tokens and targets 22,323 before adding the generated summary. The native eight-recent-group floor can prevent reaching that target. The output reserve may be smaller if the catalog model limit is smaller. These estimates are not total provider input usage. Synthetic tests cover stable request prefixes between checkpoints, a retained summary after Stop/Continue, and cancellation during summarization; they do not establish live summary quality. Sources: [RecordedCompaction.cs:21–44](../../Agent/RecordedCompaction.cs#L21-L44), [ModelCatalog.cs:126–130](../../Agent/ModelCatalog.cs#L126-L130), [ADR 0053](../adr/0053-native-summary-checkpoints.md), [HarnessSessionTests.cs:506–618](../../Mod.Tests/HarnessSessionTests.cs#L506-L618).

Each compaction records before/after semantic messages, estimated token counts, thresholds, preserved-group floor, changed flag and outcome. Model records preserve instructions, input/output messages, offered tools and returned usage. Streaming deltas include returned text and SDK-exposed reasoning; unreturned reasoning and provider-private fields are unavailable. Inspect recorded output for a concise stated rationale, not a reconstruction of private thought. City-tool records include arguments, results, simulation anchors, write evidence and returned images. Sources: [RecordedCompaction.cs:38–61](../../Agent/RecordedCompaction.cs#L38-L61), [SessionRecord.cs:151–162](../../Agent/SessionRecord.cs#L151-L162), [270–288](../../Agent/SessionRecord.cs#L270-L288), [HarnessTrace.cs:289–303](../../Agent/HarnessTrace.cs#L289-L303), [340–361](../../Agent/HarnessTrace.cs#L340-L361), [ClientSession.cs:51–53](../../Agent/ClientSession.cs#L51-L53).

## Falsifiable checks for the run

Use three expansion batches before guidance and three after it when practical. Record the initial city center, each batch's road/zone coordinates, chosen density, applicable demand/unlocks, offered catalog and simulation time. Keep the same loaded session and model connection. A second fresh-city control would better separate guidance from city maturation, but would interrupt this run's growth objective; the within-run comparison is descriptive evidence, not proof of causation.

| Question | Evidence that supports it | Evidence that limits or contradicts it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Did the mayor inspect the whole city? | A fresh citywide image, or broad and bounded text reads covering existing districts and candidate areas, before expansion; current demand, warnings, services and owned tiles. | Only local reads near the previous construction. An unavailable visual tool is a capability limit, not a missed call. |
| Did it compare real alternatives? | Returned explanation or plan identifies infill/density and distinct expansion candidates, using observed constraints to select one. | Generic promises or a new district name without alternatives and evidence. |
| Did the declared choice affect construction? | Road and zone coordinates/densities match the selected area and hierarchy; service work addresses the stated bottleneck. | The same outward corridor repeats without reassessment, or writes contradict the chosen area. Failed legal placement remains a separate explanation. |
| Is one-direction growth actually unjustified? | Repeated outward batches despite observed feasible alternatives or substantial usable empty frontage. | Ownership, water, terrain, locked density, weak demand, insufficient services or failed connections justify the selected corridor. Symmetry is not itself a planning goal. |
| Did a summary lose a city constraint? | A specific goal, protected area, rejected route or next verification present before compaction is absent or changed in the summary and next model input. | The constraint survives and is ignored later; that does not support summary loss. No checkpoint means this question is untested. |
| Did guidance change behavior? | After `input.applied`, comparisons precede writes, plans change, and the next batches use the declared city rationale. | A more detailed answer without different observations or decisions. Growth or service improvement alone does not isolate guidance's effect. |
| Did the run reach its target? | A fresh population reading/history sample at or above 100,000, with its simulation time, plus current services and warnings. | Model claims, projected population, or stale samples. `get_statistics` returns sampled history ([catalog:295](../../Mod/Host/ToolCatalog.json#L295)). |

Read `compaction.finished` together with the next ordinary mayor request. Check the 100,000 goal, player constraints, chosen district/density, rejected routes and pending verification. Compare exact semantic prefixes only between checkpoints, separately for each agent. Inspect reported input, output and cache-read usage; a stable local prefix does not guarantee provider cache hits. Sources: [HarnessTrace.cs:180–193](../../Agent/HarnessTrace.cs#L180-L193), [ADR 0053](../adr/0053-native-summary-checkpoints.md), [ADR 0054](../adr/0054-offline-recording-analysis.md). The checks above are this investigation's proposed rubric, not implemented product rules.

## Concrete player guidance

Send once after the baseline observations, preserving its exact text and `input.applied` boundary:

> Keep growing toward 100,000 residents. Before the next expansion, compare filling available frontage or using an unlocked higher density with expansion into two different directions. Read current demand, services, warnings, owned land and road connections. Use a fresh citywide map if available; otherwise compare map text covering the whole built city and the candidate areas. Choose using housing and job needs, service capacity and the possibility of more than one collector connection. Keep the chosen area, density, road roles and next verification steps in your plan. Before each expansion batch, briefly explain why this area serves the city better than the alternatives. After the batch, advance once and re-read population, demand, services and congestion. Preserve occupied neighborhoods; do not replace them merely to create variety.

This guidance makes the existing playbook observable without prescribing unsupported coordinates or requiring arbitrary rotation through directions. Distinguish a new observation, a stated choice, a successful write and a later city outcome when reporting the run. Population, warnings and congestion can change while simulation runs; temporal proximity alone does not assign their cause to the preceding write.
